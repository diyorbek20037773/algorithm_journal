Monetary Policy Transmission in Emerging Central Asian Markets: Evidence from High-Frequency Yield Curves

Dr. Alisher Karimov1, Dr. Nilufar Saidova2, Prof. David S. Thornton3

1 Center for Economic Research & Reforms, Tashkent · 2 University of Westminster in Tashkent · 3 Kiel Institute for the World Economy

Abstract Published Feb 14, 2025

This paper assesses the interest rate and exchange rate channels of monetary transmission following the recent adoption of inflation targeting regimes across emerging Central Asian economies. Utilizing a daily sovereign yield dataset spanning 2018–2024 and structural vector autoregression (SVAR) with high-frequency sign restrictions, we demonstrate that market yields respond symmetrically within a 48-hour event window, whereas bank credit channels reveal substantial institutional stickiness.
