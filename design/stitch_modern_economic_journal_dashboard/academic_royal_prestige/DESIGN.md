## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intellectual gravitas, rigorous authority, and timeless pedigree of high-impact academic and economic publications. Designed for research economists, university faculty, central bank analysts, and doctoral fellows, it marries the venerable prestige of traditional university presses with the luminous, high-velocity clarity of modern open-access digital platforms.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from **Contemporary Editorial Academicism** and **Minimalist Architectural Order**:
- **Pristine Paper Canvas:** Crisp, ultra-clean white backgrounds complemented by whisper-soft violet-tinted containers evoke heavy-weight archival stock while remaining radiant on modern screens.
- **Deep Academic Ink:** Anchored in royal ink-purple and deep plum typography, establishing unyielding contrast, uncompromising readability, and historic editorial stature without falling back on generic charcoal grays.
- **Scholarly Rigor & Restraint:** Sharp structural hairline rules, deliberate typographic tension between classical editorial serifs and analytical sans-serifs, and measured indigo-violet accents communicating peer-reviewed integrity, empirical depth, and institutional trust.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an austere, high-contrast reading environment infused with rich, saturated ink tones:

- **Primary (`#2A164D` - Deep Royal Ink Purple):** The anchor of the design system. Used for mastheads, primary article headlines, focal interactive buttons, active navigational indices, and structural anchor boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#5E35B1` - Refined Violet-Indigo):** Denotes secondary interactive hierarchy, citation tools, verified badge borders, active pagination, and scholarly taxonomy filters.
- **Tertiary (`#8E24AA` - Measured Plum Orchid):** Applied selectively for critical data highlights, cross-referenced literature markers, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#FAF9FD` - Archival Paper Crisp):** A luminous off-white with an imperceptible cool violet whisper, preventing display glare while maintaining the perception of pristine, heavy rag paper. Surfaces step up to pure `#FFFFFF` on article content sheets and elevated cards.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Headline copy renders in `royal-ink` (`#1F1435`), while long-form body text uses `plum-body` (`#2D2245`) to deliver razor-sharp contrast against pure white without the clinical harshness of synthetic pure black.

## Typography

The typography system sets up a scholarly dialogue between classical academic letterpress and crisp empirical functionalism:

- **Merriweather (Display & Headlines):** Used exclusively for journal mastheads, research titles, article abstracts, chapter subheadings, and epigraphs. It lends monumental intellectual gravity and bookish dignity.
- **Inter (Interface, Body, & Metadata):** Serves all interface scaffolding, body copy, tabular datasets, author affiliations, bibliographic footnotes, and filter controls. It delivers neutral legibility at dense data scales.
- **Micro-Copy Rules:** Labels, JEL classification tags, and DOI links must always use `label-md` or `label-sm` set in uppercase tracking (+0.05em to +0.06em) to evoke archival catalog stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a **12-column fixed-max hybrid editorial grid** constrained to an optimal reading maximum of 1360px.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) lateral margins. Reading pages deploy an asymmetric structural split: 8 columns dedicated to clean, unimpeded research text and 4 columns for metadata, citation figures, JEL tags, and figure references.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Collateral sidebars collapse into structured metadata accordions placed above or below the abstract.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Content shifts into a unified vertical flow with sticky table-of-contents jump drawers.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8px base grid, maintaining strict baseline alignment across multi-column scholarly comparisons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Architectural Tonal Layering and Crisp Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than synthetic blur or heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** The global background rests on `#FAF9FD`.
- **Containers & Document Sheets:** Journal article viewports, data cards, and search filters sit on `#FFFFFF` and are bounded by razor-thin 1px `#E7E0F2` borders.
- **Layered Accents:** Inset sidebars, methodology callouts, and mathematical formulas utilize `#F3EFFB` surfaces with no additional elevation.
- **Interactive Focus:** On mouse hover, research preview cards do not float dramatically; they project a subtle ink-tinted ambient glow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(42, 22, 77, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(42, 22, 77, 0.03)`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Citation export sheets, peer-review reports, and manuscript submission dialogs use a defined structural shadow: `0 20px 28px -6px rgba(31, 20, 53, 0.14), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(31, 20, 53, 0.06)` with a 1px border in `#D5CAE6`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language with subtle, disciplined corner treatments:

- Default buttons, input fields, search filters, and article cards use a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Badges, DOI pills, JEL codes, and verification stamps utilize an ultra-fine `0.125rem` (2px) to `0.25rem` (4px) radius, preserving the aesthetic of stamped academic print records.
- Large floating modals or lateral slide-out drawers use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Continuous rounded-pill and organic bubble shapes are strictly prohibited to preserve institutional authority and structural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Royal Ink (`#2A164D`) fill, crisp white Inter text (weight 600), 4px border radius. Padding: 10px vertical, 20px horizontal. Hover transitions to `#4A1D7A`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, crisp 1px border in `#2A164D`, ink purple text. Hover introduces a delicate `#F3EFFB` surface wash.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Borderless, 14px Inter text in `#5E35B1` with subtle baseline transition on hover; used for "Cite", "Export BibTeX", and "Share".

### Chips & Scholarly Badges
- **Open Access Indicator:** `#EBF7F1` background, 1px `#B8E5D1` border, `#1B6E4A` text.
- **DOI & e-ISSN Stamp:** `#F3EFFB` container, 1px `#E7E0F2` border, `#2A164D` monospaced or uppercase sans-serif text.
- **Peer-Reviewed / Editor's Pick:** `#FEF7ED` background, 1px `#FCD9BD` border, `#9C5700` amber text.

### Research Cards & Article Indexes
- Elevated on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E7E0F2` architectural frame.
- Structured header featuring volume, issue number, and JEL classification tags.
- Article titles set in `headline-md` Merriweather (`#1F1435`), followed by author strings with superscript affiliation indices.
- Footers integrate citation statistics, direct PDF download triggers, and DOI copy tools separated by a 1px hairline divider.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background in pure `#FFFFFF`, enclosed in a 1px `#D5CAE6` border with a 4px corner radius.
- Placeholder copy in muted plum (`#7E7294`).
- Active focus invokes a solid 1px `#2A164D` border paired with an ambient 3px ink-tinted ring (`rgba(74, 29, 122, 0.12)`). Includes integrated keyboard shortcuts (`⌘K`) and structured filter dropdowns for JEL categories and date horizons.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom 16px controls with 2px borders in `#D5CAE6`.
- Active selection fills completely with `#2A164D` featuring a centered white check icon. Focus states leverage a 2px outer glow in `#E7E0F2`.

### Tabular Data & Empirical Displays
- High-density academic tables featuring 1px `#E7E0F2` horizontal borders, zero vertical striping, and uppercase `label-sm` column headers in `#1F1435`. Numeric columns right-align with tabular lining figures.
