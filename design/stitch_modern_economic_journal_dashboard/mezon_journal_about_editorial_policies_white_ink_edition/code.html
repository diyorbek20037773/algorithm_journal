Institutional Profile

Advancing rigorous, policy-relevant economic discovery across Central Asia and beyond.

MEZON: Review of Economic Research is an international, peer-reviewed, diamond open-access scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-impact, original research across all facets of modern economics. Published monthly, the journal offers a premier multilingual platform accepting high-caliber submissions in English, Uzbek, and Russian.

Founded with the objective of bridging empirical rigor and actionable economic policy, MEZON provides immediate, barrier-free access to scholars, central banks, developmental institutions, and public policymakers. We operate on the principle of academic equity: neither authors nor readers are charged fees at any stage of the editorial process.

What We Publish

Methodologically sound empirical and theoretical scholarship relevant to emerging markets and transition economics.

We publish empirical and theoretical work that is methodologically sound, clearly written, and relevant beyond a single national context. Studies of Uzbekistan, Central Asia, and other emerging and transition economies are especially welcome when the questions they ask and the methods they deploy carry broad international significance.

Our editors prize contributions that apply reproducible micro-econometric methods, macro-structural modelling, and contemporary behavioral insights to pressing challenges: financial inclusion, green energy transitions, regional supply chains, and fiscal decentralization.

How We Work

Our transparent, rigorous 5-stage editorial review system adheres to the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) core practices.

1. Desk Screening & Plagiarism Check

Within 5 Days

Every manuscript is screened by the Managing Editor for scope alignment, academic integrity, and similarity index using Crossref Similarity Check (iThenticate). Submissions failing originality standards are rejected immediately.

2. Double-Blind Peer Review

21 – 28 Days

Manuscripts passing desk review are assigned to a Handling Associate Editor who commissions at least two independent expert reviewers. Author identities and reviewer identities are masked at every step of exchange.

3. Editorial Decision & Revision Rounds

Standard 30 Days for Revisions

Authors receive constructive referee reports with one of four outcomes: Accept, Minor Revisions, Major Revisions, or Reject. Revised papers undergo review verification by the handling editor.

4. Data & Code Replication Audit

Within 7 Days of Acceptance

To guarantee scientific reproducibility, authors of empirical papers must submit replication packages (Stata, R, Python scripts and clean anonymized datasets). Our data editor replicates all main econometric tables.

5. Proofreading, DOI Assignment & Online First

Immediate Digital Release

Accepted manuscripts receive professional layout XML/PDF typesetting and a registered Crossref DOI. Articles publish immediately under "Online First" ahead of formal monthly issue inclusion.
