## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intellectual gravity, rigor, and heritage of world-renowned scholarly publishers like Oxford University Press, Cambridge University Press, and NBER, synthesised with the modern clarity, speed, and open-access transparency of Nature. 

The target audience comprises research economists, university faculty, doctoral researchers, central bank policymakers, and quantitative analysts who demand uncompromised typographic legibility, instant access to empirical datasets and citation tools, and clear verification metadata.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Scholarly Editorial** with **Corporate Precision**:
- **Monastic Clarity:** Generous, calm reading environments with crisp alabaster/paper backgrounds free of superficial noise.
- **Architectural Hierarchy:** Strong structural hairline dividers, precise grid alignments, and distinct typography pairings between classical editorial serifs and functional metadata sans-serifs.
- **Academic Distinction:** Strategic use of deep archival navy grounded by warm, measured gold and amber accents signifying editorial validation, high-impact metrics, peer-review badges, and institutional trust.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an environment of focused reading and authoritative prestige. 

- **Primary (`#0F2537` - Archival Academic Navy):** Represents scholarly permanence and editorial authority. Used for major mastheads, primary publication titles, active navigation states, primary CTA buttons, and prominent structural anchor points.
- **Secondary (`#C28B38` - Burnished Gold / Amber):** Represents academic excellence, citation impact factor highlights, open-access insignias, editor's choice badges, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#334155` - Deep Slate):** Delivers articulate contrast for body copy, author affiliations, secondary headings, and publication metadata labels.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Alabaster Paper):** Evokes crisp, heavy-weight archival paper stock. Supported by pure `#FFFFFF` for elevated journal cards, reading sheets, and article containers, framed by delicate borders (`#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`).

### Semantic Alert & Status Roles
- **Success / Open Access:** `#15803D` (Subtle Forest Green, badge backgrounds tint at 8% opacity).
- **In Review / Revision:** `#B45309` (Muted Ochre).
- **Archival / Withdrawn:** `#B91C1C` (Academic Crimson).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an intentional dialectic between classical publishing and contemporary digital scholarship.

- **Scholarly Serif (Merriweather):** Reserved strictly for mastheads, article titles, section manifestos, and lead quotes. It provides dignity, intellectual weight, and a tangible sense of peer-reviewed permanence.
- **Precision Sans-Serif (Inter):** Deployed for abstracts, navigation bars, author affiliations, citations, filter panels, tabular data, and metrics. Delivers effortless legibility across complex multi-column layouts and compact meta badges.
- **Monospaced Numerical System (JetBrains Mono):** Applied selectively to DOIs, e-ISSN markers, JEL classification codes, math formulas, and citation copy snippets.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal is built upon a **12-column fixed-max hybrid grid** with a maximum content bound of 1360px, maintaining balanced reading proportions across expansive desktop displays.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) page boundary margins. Allows optimal two-column editorial composition: 8 columns for primary research articles and 4 columns for metadata, citation figures, related articles, and editorial boards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Sidebars fold into contextual accordions beneath abstracts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral margins. Stacks navigation, search controls, and article indexes into continuous vertical streams.

All vertical rhythms adhere strictly to an 8px baseline grid with standard 4px sub-intervals for inline metadata tokens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or skeuomorphic bevels; instead, it utilizes **Tonal Layering and Low-Contrast Architectural Outlines**.

1. **Base Layer:** The overarching viewport rests on `#F8FAFC` (Alabaster neutral).
2. **Elevated Card Layer:** Active article cards, submission drawers, and data filter panels sit on pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by hairline 1px `#E2E8F0` borders.
3. **Subtle Interaction Shadows:** Interactive hover states on research paper cards introduce a feather-light, tinted elevation: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.03)`.
4. **Modals & Flyouts:** Dropdowns, language selectors, and submission upload modals use a deep structural shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 37, 55, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The portal adopts a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape language to reinforce intellectual discipline and formal authority:

- Standard buttons, input search fields, cards, and dropdown containers use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radiuses.
- Badges (DOI tags, CC BY 4.0 status, JEL codes, peer-review verification) utilize a clean `0.125rem` to `0.25rem` radius, preserving their resemblance to crisp editorial stamps.
- Avoid pill or bubble shapes entirely; rounded pills erode the academic dignity of the journal portal.

## Components

### 1. Header & Institutional Navigation
- Primary masthead with bold serif title paired with an architectural bottom rule (`2px solid #0F2537`).
- Secondary utility bar housing multi-lingual toggles (EN, UZ, O'Z, RU), search triggers, and direct-action buttons.
- Search input accompanied by a clear keyboard shortcut hint (`⌘K`) and structured filter dropdown (JEL codes, Author, Date).

### 2. Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Button:** Solid Archival Navy (`#0F2537`) with crisp white typography, 4px radius, 10px vertical and 20px horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#1A3854`.
- **Secondary / Guidelines Button:** Crisp 1px border (`#0F2537` or `#CBD5E1`), transparent background, navy text. Hover applies subtle neutral wash (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Accent Action:** Used for "Submit Manuscript" or "Download Citation", adopting subtle gold hairline underlines or amber highlights.

### 3. Metadata Badges & Scholarly Tags
- **DOI & e-ISSN Pill:** Monospaced font, subtle light slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), slate border (`#E2E8F0`), slate text (`#475569`).
- **Open Access Badge:** Subtle emerald wash (`#ECFDF5`), forest border (`#A7F3D0`), rich green text (`#15803D`).
- **Impact & Peer-Review Pill:** Amber wash (`#FFFBEB`), gold border (`#FDE68A`), dark amber text (`#92400E`).

### 4. Article & Research Cards
- Clean white surface with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Clear taxonomy hierarchy: Issue date & JEL classification at top, bold Merriweather article title in primary navy, author string with affiliation superscript, 2-line condensed abstract, and bottom metadata row (Citations, Downloads, PDF button, DOI link).

### 5. Tabs & Research Filters
- Underline tab styling with a 2px active indicator in Archival Navy (`#0F2537`). Inactive tabs remain muted slate (`#64748B`).
- Sticky lateral facet filter column with collapsible checkboxes, clear selection counts, and numerical frequency badges.

### 6. Inputs & Form Fields
- Neutral light gray background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px outline in `#CBD5E1`, transitioning to `#0F2537` on active focus with a subtle 2px ring offset in `#E2E8F0`.
